## Steps

Great-grandma made this on a Sunday. You're going to make it on a Tuesday.

1. Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the mushrooms and cook 4 minutes until they're browned and the water's cooked off.
2. Sprinkle the flour over the mushrooms. Stir for 30 seconds — it should look pasty.
3. Pour in the chicken broth while stirring, then the cream. Bring to a simmer. It'll thicken in about 2 minutes.
4. Add the shredded chicken, frozen peas, and pimentos. Stir to coat. Simmer 3 more minutes to heat everything through. Season with salt and lots of black pepper.
5. Toast the bread. Ladle the chicken over the toast, two slices per plate.

## Notes

- **The original 1920s version** used chicken cooked from scratch and served the mixture in a puff pastry shell (a "vol-au-vent"). Rotisserie + toast is a cheat that's honestly better on a weeknight.
- **No mushrooms?** Skip them — the dish is still great. Or sub 1/2 cup of chopped cooked bacon.
- **Sherry version:** stir in 2 tbsp dry sherry with the cream. Classic and worth it if you have some.
- **Over rice or egg noodles** if you're out of bread. Or biscuits. Very much a "use what you have" recipe.
