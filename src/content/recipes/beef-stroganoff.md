## Steps

Get the noodle water going first. Everything else stacks in one pan.

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil for the noodles.
2. Meanwhile, heat the olive oil in a skillet over high heat. Sprinkle the sirloin with salt and pepper. Sear it 90 seconds per side — you want a crust, not to cook it through. Move to a plate.
3. Drop the noodles into the boiling water. Cook per package (usually 6 minutes).
4. In the same skillet, melt 2 tbsp butter over medium-high. Add the mushrooms and onion. Cook 5 minutes until browned and soft. Add the garlic in the last 30 seconds.
5. Sprinkle the flour over. Stir 30 seconds. Pour in the beef broth. Simmer 2 minutes until thickened.
6. Off the heat, stir in the sour cream and mustard. Return the beef and any resting juices to the pan. Season with salt and pepper.
7. Drain the noodles. Toss with the last 1 tbsp butter. Plate the noodles, spoon the stroganoff over, top with parsley.

## Notes

- **The heat matters.** Off-heat when adding sour cream — otherwise it curdles. Not the end of the world if it does; still tastes right.
- **Cheaper cut option:** flat iron steak works and is often half the price of sirloin.
- **No sirloin?** Ground beef works — brown it in step 2 instead. Different dish, still called stroganoff, still delicious.
- **Leftovers reheat gently** with a splash of broth. Don't microwave-blast — it separates.
