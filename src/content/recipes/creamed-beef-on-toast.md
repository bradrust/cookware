## Steps

There are five ingredients. There are five steps. There is no complication anywhere in this recipe.

1. Rinse the dried beef quickly under warm water to knock down some of the salt. Pat dry. Tear into bite-size pieces.
2. Melt the butter in a skillet over medium. Add the flour. Whisk 60 seconds until pale golden.
3. Whisk in the milk in a slow steady stream. Bring to a simmer, whisking constantly, until thickened — about 2 minutes.
4. Stir in the dried beef. Simmer 2 more minutes. Add a *lot* of black pepper. Do not add salt — the beef brings all of it.
5. Toast the bread. Ladle the creamed beef over. Two slices per plate. Done.

## Notes

- **Dried beef** lives near the tuna or in the deli aisle. Brands: Armour, Buddig, Hormel. Cheap.
- **Kids like this dish more than they'll admit.** The name is unpromising; the flavor is butter-milk-salt, which they eat willingly under other names.
- **Add a fried egg on top** and it's a Sunday-morning-adjacent dinner.
- **Bacon works instead of dried beef** — cook 6 slices, chop, use the same technique.
