## Steps

Two pans running side-by-side. Soup in one, sandwiches in the other.

**Soup:**
1. Melt 2 tbsp butter in a medium pot over medium heat. Add the onion, cook 4 minutes until soft.
2. Add the tomatoes, broth, sugar, salt, and pepper. Simmer 8 minutes.
3. Off the heat, blend smooth with an immersion blender. Stir in the cream. Taste — more salt or a pinch more sugar if needed.

**Grilled cheese** (while the soup simmers):
1. Butter one side of every slice of bread — 4 tbsp total for the bread.
2. In a hot skillet over medium, place 4 slices butter-side-down. Top each with cheese and a second slice, butter-side up.
3. Cook 3 minutes per side until golden and the cheese has melted.

Serve immediately. The soup can wait a few minutes; the sandwich can't.

## Notes

- **San Marzano tomatoes are the difference.** They cost $4 a can vs $2 for generic and it's obvious in the finished soup. Worth it.
- **Not vegetarian if using chicken broth** — swap in vegetable broth or water.
- **For a fancier version:** stir 1 tbsp of sherry into the soup off the heat. Very 1925.
- **Dunking is required. Not optional.**
