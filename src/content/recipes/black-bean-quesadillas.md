## Steps

If you can flip a grilled cheese, you can do this.

1. Drain and rinse the black beans. Dump them in a bowl. Mash lightly with a fork — leaving some whole. Stir in the cumin, salt, and 2 tbsp of the salsa.
2. Slice the green onions.
3. Heat a dry skillet over medium. Lay one tortilla flat. Spread ¼ of the bean mix on half of it. Sprinkle with cheese and green onions. Fold in half.
4. Cook 2 minutes per side until the tortilla is golden and the cheese has melted. Move to a plate. Repeat.
5. Serve with the remaining salsa (and sour cream if you have it).

## Notes

- **Add rotisserie chicken** to any that aren't going to a vegetarian eater. Two dinners from one skillet.
- **Frozen corn** stirred into the bean mix is another 90 seconds of cook time and doubles the volume.
- **Kids will pick out green onions.** Chop them small or skip.
- **Leftovers** hold in the fridge 2 days. Reheat in the skillet, not the microwave (they go soggy).
