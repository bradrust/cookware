## Steps

The whole "recipe" is: cut things, toss with oil, bake.

1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Line a sheet pan with parchment (saves the cleanup).
2. Halve the baby potatoes. Slice the sausage into ½-inch coins. Cut the pepper into strips.
3. Toss everything on the sheet pan with the olive oil, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
4. Roast 20 minutes. Halfway through, stir once with a spatula so nothing sticks.
5. Done. Serve straight from the pan.

## Notes

- **Chicken sausage** works fine if you want less fatty. Roast an extra 3 minutes.
- **Frozen broccoli** works too — don't thaw, just spread it out so it doesn't steam.
- **Leftovers** reheat in the air fryer for 4 minutes at 375°F and taste new again.
