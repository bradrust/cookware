## Steps

The whole secret is a hot pan and not stirring too much. Let the bottom crust.

1. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a cast-iron skillet over medium-high. Add the frozen potatoes and onion. Sprinkle with salt.
2. Cook 6 minutes, stirring only twice, until the potatoes are getting crispy and golden on the bottom.
3. Crumble the canned corned beef into the pan with your hands or a fork. Add the Worcestershire. Stir once. Press down with the spatula to help the whole mixture crisp on the bottom, 3 more minutes.
4. Meanwhile, fry the eggs in a separate small skillet in the remaining 1 tbsp oil until whites are set but yolks are runny.
5. Divide the hash between plates. Top each with a fried egg. Hot sauce over the top if you like it.

## Notes

- **Canned corned beef** lives near the tuna. Libby's is standard. It's a $4 can that becomes dinner for four.
- **The runny yolk is the sauce.** Do not overcook the eggs. This is the whole dish.
- **Leftovers reheat well** in the skillet — get it good and crispy the second time too. Under a fried egg the next morning = breakfast.
- **For a fancy version**, add 2 tbsp of chopped fresh chives before serving. Just kidding — leave it alone. It's perfect.
