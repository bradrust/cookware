## Steps

You mix a bowl, you pour it in a pan, you bake it. That's the recipe.

1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Grease a loaf pan with the olive oil.
2. In a bowl, flake the drained salmon with a fork — bones and skin are fine, they mash right in and add calcium.
3. Add the crushed crackers, eggs, diced onion, parsley, milk, the zest and juice of the lemon, plus salt and pepper.
4. Stir until it comes together as a slightly wet mixture. Press into the loaf pan.
5. Bake 25 minutes. Let rest 5 minutes before slicing.

Serve with lemon wedges and something green.

## Notes

- **Canned pink salmon** is what your great-grandma used and it's cheaper than tuna. Look for the label "wild Alaskan pink." Costco's Kirkland brand is great.
- **The lemon matters** — do not skip. Both zest and juice, or the loaf tastes flat.
- **Leftovers keep** 3 days. Slice cold onto toast with tomato and mayo — that's tomorrow's lunch.
- **Kids balking at "salmon loaf"?** Call it "salmon burgers" and shape into patties. Bake 15 minutes on a sheet pan instead.
