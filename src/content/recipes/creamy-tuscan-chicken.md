## Steps

Nothing tricky. If you can cook a piece of chicken, you can do this.

1. Season chicken thighs on both sides with salt and pepper.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Add the chicken and cook 4 minutes per side, until golden. Move to a plate — it will finish cooking in the sauce.
3. In the same pan, add the garlic (roughly chopped, no need to mince) and the sun-dried tomatoes with a spoonful of their oil. Stir 30 seconds.
4. Pour in the cream. Let it bubble for 2 minutes until slightly thickened.
5. Stir in the spinach — it wilts almost immediately.
6. Return the chicken to the pan. Simmer 2 more minutes to warm through.

Serve over pasta, rice, or crusty bread. Or straight from the pan — no one's judging.

## Notes

- **Chicken breasts work** if that's what you have. Slice them in half horizontally so they cook faster.
- **No sun-dried tomatoes?** Swap in a small jar of roasted red peppers, drained and chopped.
- **Leftovers keep** 3 days in the fridge. The sauce actually gets better.
