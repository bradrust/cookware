## Steps

Nothing here you couldn't do while helping a kid with homework.

1. Brown the ground beef in a skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it up. Cook 5 minutes.
2. Add the onion and green pepper. Cook 5 more minutes until softened. Drain off excess fat if there's a puddle.
3. Add everything else: ketchup, mustard, brown sugar, vinegar, Worcestershire, salt, and lots of pepper.
4. Stir. Simmer 5–7 minutes on medium-low until it thickens into a spoonable sauce.
5. Pile on toasted buns.

## Notes

- **Ground turkey works** if you want to lighten it. Add an extra tablespoon of oil to the pan since it's leaner.
- **Green pepper haters:** dice it fine or skip. The dish forgives.
- **Leftovers over rice** the next night = an entirely different-feeling dinner from the same pot.
- **Freezes beautifully.** Double the batch, freeze half in a quart bag flat, thaw + reheat any weeknight.
