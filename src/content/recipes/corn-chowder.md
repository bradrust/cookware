## Steps

The whole thing is a one-pot job. You cook the bacon, everything else piles in on top.

1. Cook the bacon in a large pot over medium heat until crisp, 5 minutes. Fish out most of the bacon with a slotted spoon and set aside. Leave 2 tbsp fat in the pot.
2. Add the onion and diced potatoes to the pot. Cook 3 minutes, stirring.
3. Pour in the milk. Bring to a bare simmer (don't boil — it'll break). Simmer 8 minutes until potatoes are tender.
4. Stir in both cans of corn, most of the bacon (save some for topping), salt, pepper, and paprika. Simmer 2 more minutes to heat through.
5. Ladle into bowls. Top with the reserved bacon.

## Notes

- **Frozen corn works** instead of canned — use 2 cups. Add with the milk so it thaws in the pot.
- **Creamier?** Sub 1 cup of the milk for heavy cream. Very 1920s.
- **For lunch tomorrow:** thicken any leftovers with a spoonful of instant mashed potatoes. Corn chowder loves next-day.
- **Vegetarian version:** skip the bacon, sauté the onion in 2 tbsp butter, add 1/2 tsp smoked paprika for the missing smoke note.
