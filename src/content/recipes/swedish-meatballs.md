## Steps

The whole recipe is: make a creamy gravy, dump the meatballs in, done.

1. Bring a pot of salted water to a boil for noodles. Cook per package while making the gravy.
2. Melt the butter in a skillet over medium. Whisk in the flour. Cook 60 seconds until pale gold.
3. Whisk in the beef broth in a slow steady stream. Bring to a simmer — it thickens in about 2 minutes.
4. Add the frozen meatballs straight from the bag. Cover the skillet and simmer 10 minutes to heat them through.
5. Uncover. Stir in the cream, sour cream, Dijon, Worcestershire, nutmeg, salt, and pepper. Simmer 2 more minutes.
6. Serve over egg noodles or mashed potatoes. Top with parsley if you have it.

## Notes

- **The nutmeg is what makes it "Swedish."** It sounds weird. It's essential. Don't skip.
- **Fresh meatballs work too** — cook them first in the skillet, remove, then build the gravy in the same pan.
- **Mashed potato pouches** (Idahoan, Bob Evans) are a perfectly respectable serving base — dinner ready in 20 minutes total.
- **Cranberry sauce on the side** is the traditional Swedish pairing. Sounds odd, tastes right.
