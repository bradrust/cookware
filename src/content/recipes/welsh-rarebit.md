## Steps

The whole thing is a cheese sauce and a toast. Nothing more.

1. Turn the broiler to high. Toast the bread on a sheet pan, one side only, until golden. Flip.
2. Meanwhile, melt the butter in a small saucepan over medium. Whisk in the flour and cook 30 seconds.
3. Whisk in the milk (and beer if using) until smooth. Bring to a bare simmer.
4. Off the heat, stir in the cheese, mustard, Worcestershire, cayenne, and salt. Stir until melted and smooth. It should be thick enough to coat a spoon.
5. Spoon generously over the toasted-side-up bread. Broil 60–90 seconds until bubbly and browned in spots. Watch it — it burns fast.

## Notes

- **The beer is traditional and adds savory depth**, but milk-only works fine, especially for kids.
- **Skip Worcestershire for strict vegetarian** — it usually contains anchovy. Use a splash of soy sauce instead.
- **Fried egg on top** turns this into "Buck Rarebit" — Sunday brunch move.
- **Extra sauce keeps** 3 days in the fridge. Reheat gently with a splash of milk, use on nachos, mac, or steamed broccoli.
