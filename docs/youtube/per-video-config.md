# Per-Video Upload Checklist

Every video, every time. Skipping any of these leaves conversion on the table. Print this or paste into a Notion template so it becomes a shoot-day-adjacent habit.

## Pre-Upload (Before You Open YouTube Studio)

- [ ] Final video file exported (H.264, 4K UHD, 45–65 Mbps, 24 or 30 fps to match source)
- [ ] Thumbnail file ready (1280×720 PNG, <2 MB)
- [ ] 30-sec Shorts cut exported separately (vertical 1080×1920, captions burned in)
- [ ] Recipe published on `shopthisrecipe.com` — you need the URL for the description
- [ ] Affiliate short-links (`short.io` branded links) generated for the retailers you want to feature
- [ ] Playlist decisions made — which of the [10 initial playlists](playlists.md) does this video belong in? (usually 3–4)

## Title

Under 60 characters. Follows patterns from your private plans repo (`cookware-plans/content-strategy/seo-youtube.md`). Examples:

- `15-Minute Creamy Tuscan Chicken (One Pan, 5 Ingredients)`
- `Rotisserie Chicken → 4 Weeknight Dinners`
- `Sheet Pan Sausage & Veg (5 Minutes of Effort)`

## Tags

10–15 tags. Most-specific first, broadest last.

**Baseline (paste into every video):**
```
easy dinner recipes, quick dinner ideas, weeknight cooking, 15 minute meals, shop this recipe
```

**Then add 5–10 video-specific:**
```
creamy tuscan chicken, one pan chicken, chicken thighs recipe, italian chicken recipe, ...
```

Don't stuff unrelated trending tags — YouTube's algorithm penalizes this.

## Chapters (Non-Negotiable)

Chapters improve retention (viewers re-navigate) and searchability (each chapter is indexed separately). Add 5+ chapters per video.

**Rules for chapters to actually work:**
1. First chapter timestamp must be `00:00`
2. Each chapter minimum 10 seconds long
3. At least 3 chapters total
4. Add them in the description like this:

```
00:00 The dish
00:15 Ingredients
01:00 Cooking
04:30 Plating
05:45 Tasting
```

YouTube auto-detects the format and enables the chapter progress bar.

## Thumbnail

1280×720 PNG. Follows the thumbnail rules in your private plans repo (`cookware-plans/content-strategy/seo-youtube.md`). Test with the built-in Thumbnail Test feature — upload 2 variants and let YouTube pick a winner over the first 2 weeks.

## Upload Settings

### Details tab

- **Title:** as above
- **Description:** as above
- **Thumbnail:** custom upload
- **Playlists:** check every applicable one (see [playlists.md](playlists.md))
- **Audience:** No, not made for kids
- **Restrictions:** No (unless the recipe has real ones)

### Show more

- **Altered content:** No — UNLESS this is an AI face/body swap video. If AI-swapped, YES. See the synthetic-media policy notes in your private plans repo (`cookware-plans/legal/youtube-synthetic-media.md`).
- **Tags:** as above
- **Language:** English
- **Captions certification:** none required
- **Recording date:** actual shoot date (helps analytics differentiate cohorts)
- **Video location:** blank
- **License:** Standard YouTube License
- **Category:** How-to & Style
- **Comments:** Allow all
- **Show how many viewers like:** Yes

### Video elements tab

- **Add end screen** — use a template:
  - Left: "Latest video" (auto)
  - Right: "Subscribe" watermark
  - Bottom: "Best for viewer" (auto-suggest based on watch history)
- **Add cards** — 1 card at the ~4:30 mark linking to a related video

### Checks tab

- Wait for copyright / partner content checks to complete before publishing (usually <1 min)

### Visibility tab

- **Schedule** for prime time (4–7 PM ET typically best for food, but check your specific YouTube Analytics)
- **Publish to Subscriptions feed:** Yes
- **Instant Premiere:** No (Premieres are for large channels; wastes early impressions for small ones)

## Post-Upload (First 5 Minutes)

- [ ] **Pin the affiliate CTA comment.** Copy this template:
  ```
  🛒 Everything for this recipe in one click → [shopthisrecipe.com/your-slug]
  Instacart, Walmart, or Amazon Fresh — pick your grocer. Thanks for supporting the channel! 🙌
  ```
- [ ] Publish the corresponding **Community tab post** — a still from the video + "New recipe tonight! Recipe card in comments."
- [ ] Upload the **Shorts cut** as a separate short — link back to the long-form in the Shorts description
- [ ] **Cross-post the Shorts cut** to Instagram Reels and TikTok
- [ ] Set a reminder to check the video's 24-hour analytics — first-day CTR and avg watch % predict the video's ceiling

## Post-Upload (First 24 Hours)

- [ ] **Reply to every comment.** Massive engagement lift, especially on comments in the first hour.
- [ ] **Heart top comments** as they land.
- [ ] Check the **Thumbnail Test** result at 48 hours (if enabled).
- [ ] Review the **first-24-hour analytics**:
  - CTR ≥ 5% = the thumbnail is working
  - Avg viewed % ≥ 45% = the video is working
  - Watch time from Suggested / Search = the algorithm is engaging with it

## Post-Upload (First Week)

- [ ] Add the video to any playlists you missed at upload
- [ ] If the video hits >10K views, consider adding a **manual card** at the drop-off point (from analytics) linking to a stronger related video
- [ ] Send the video to your email list on Sunday (batch)
- [ ] Pin the video to your channel homepage if it's a strong performer

## What NOT To Do

- **Don't skip the pinned comment.** It drives affiliate CTR 3–5x vs description-only.
- **Don't manually delete negative comments** unless they violate community rules. Deleting drops the engagement signal; hearting positive ones is better than fighting the trolls.
- **Don't edit the title in the first 48 hours.** YouTube's algorithm treats the initial title as the promise made to viewers; changing it re-scores the video.
- **Don't upload without picking playlists.** Every video *should* be in ≥ 3 playlists at upload time — retrofitting later loses first-day session-time gains.
- **Don't use the same thumbnail style as every other food channel.** Amy's face + hero food + big time-promise text = your identity. Vary the composition weekly; keep the identity constant.
